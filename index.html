Bias Spotter Challenge

Train yourself to identify unfair bias in AI-generated text.

Welcome to the Bias Spotter Challenge!

Now that you've seen the video on AI limitations, this activity will help you practice and reinforce what you've learned. The goal is to get better at spotting the subtle (and not-so-subtle) ways that AI can reflect unfair social biases.

How it Works:

  • You will be presented with three AI-generated case studies.
  • Read the text and click on any words or phrases that seem biased.
  • Once you highlight something, select the reason why you think it's biased.
  • Review the instant feedback to see the explanation and sharpen your skills.

Next Steps & Resources

Continue to build your AI literacy. Here are some practical steps and high-value resources:

  • Experiment with Prompts: Actively try to write prompts that avoid bias. See how changing a few words in a prompt can alter the AI's output.
  • Stay Curious: The world of AI is changing fast. Follow reputable sources to stay informed about new tools and ethical discussions.
